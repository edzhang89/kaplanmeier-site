The Advisory

Kaplan Meier is a private travel advisory for physicians and their families, run by physicians. We know what it takes to actually leave: the call coverage, the block schedule negotiated months out, the ten days you refuse to waste. We plan around all of it.

Each itinerary is designed from a blank page, bespoke to the way you travel: solo after a hard stretch, a couple finally off service at the same time, or the whole family over spring break. We take care of the research, the reservations, and the details in between, so a week away actually feels like one.

Partnerships

We book through preferred partnerships with the world’s finest hotel brands, including Four Seasons, Rosewood, Belmond, and Aman, along with several hundred more. When we place you at a partner property, the stay comes with more than a reservation:

  • A room upgrade, when one can be found
  • Breakfast for two, daily
  • A property credit, usually a hundred dollars toward the spa or a dinner
  • Early check-in and late check-out, where the house can manage it
  • A warmer welcome: the property knows you are our client before you arrive
  • A consult line: if anything comes up mid-trip, call us and we will work it through with the property on your behalf

The rate is the same one you would pay booking directly. The stay is not.

Referrals

Our client list stays intentionally short, so new clients join by introduction. We take on a limited number of families each quarter. If someone sent you our way, we would love to hear from you: ask them to make the connection, or write to referrals@kaplanmeier.com and tell us who referred you.
